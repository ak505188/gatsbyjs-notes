# Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 Any% Restricted
### Notation
For menus, I will sometimes have something in parenthesis. If it's (S1-3), it refers to the slot the Picto should be placed in. If it's a Playstation controller button, it's a button you should press to optimize the menu.

For battles, there will sometimes be something like (1.1 QTE). This refers to the Quick Time Action you do when you use an Ability. A perfect is 1.2, a normal is 1.1, and a fail is 1.0. The number refers to the damage multiplier the skill gets from the QTE.

For loading saves, I will use notation like load S0 to load the top save, or S2 to load the 3rd save (2 down).
These will assume no mistakes, since mistakes will often lead to extra saves. The notes will still describe which save to load.

There's also a debug reload you can do by pressing Alt+V. I will refer to this as **Debug Load**. You generally have to mash it as you enter an area, exit a menu, or load screen is ending.

---

You can check the changelog at the bottom of the notes.

These are my personal notes for the category, the writing will reflect that. For the most part these notes will not say where to go or where things are, just what to do. I will be updating these as the route progresses, changes will be reflected in the changelog at the bottom of the notes.

There will also be enemy data and maybe some other useful information at the bottom of the notes.

If there's a time listed by a fight, it's my best time for it so far. It does not represent the average.

#### Paintress (3:47)
> 173736XP, $8520, Painted Power, Resplendant x3

Counter everything unless otherwise stated.

> It's possible to both over-damage and under-damage.
> To prevent over-damage, if you crit both Offensive Switch and Immolation, Fail QTE Ice Lance.
> If you crit all hits of the 1st AoE counter, Fail QTE Lightning Dance
> For underdamage, if you didn't crit Offensive Switch, Immolation, and any of Verso & Maelle counters, Maelle shoots Paintress body twice.

##### Turn 0
- Maelle: Offensive Switch
- Lune: Immolation
- Verso: Energy Tint Lune

> If you crit both Immolation and Swift Stride, consider tuning damage by failing Ice Lance and/or Lightning Dance QTEs. There is a chance you overdamage with the 4th counter, which slows down the fight significantly. I don't know where the exact thresholds are yet.

Dodge Meteors.

##### Turn 1
- Lune: Ice Lance
- Maelle: Rain of Fire
- Verso: Berserk Slash

##### Turn 2
- Lune: Lightning Dance
- Maelle: Rain of Fire

#### Phase 2
> It's possible to under-damage here, or replace the Berserk Slash with Defiant Strike if damage is good.
> If you've gotten 0 bonus damage, add 2 Verso shots. Untested, need 1761 extra damage.

> For the Defiant Strike bonus, you need 13378 bonus damage, 11760 if gambling on 2nd Defiant Strike crit.
> The 2nd Defiant Strike crit can be replaced by 2 Lune headshots.

> Here are the damage numbers you need to count bonus damage.
> Defiant 1 Crit: +1619
> Counter 1 min: 13462
> Rain of Fire min: 11437
> Counter 2 min: 11644

##### Turn 2.5
- Verso: Defiant Strike

##### Turn 3
- Lune: Wildfire
- Maelle: Rain of Fire
- Verso: Berserk Slash

##### Turn 4
- Lune: Lightning Dance

#### Phase 3
